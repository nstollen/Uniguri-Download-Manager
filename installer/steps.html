Add UDM to your browser

The UDM downloader is running: its hedgehog icon is near the clock. One last thing, done once: load the extension.

  1. Open the extensions page

    Type this in the address bar:

    chrome://extensions

    In Brave: brave://extensions, in Edge: edge://extensions

  2. Turn on Developer mode

    The switch is at the top right (in Edge, on the left).

  3. Click Load unpacked and pick this folder

    It is open in Explorer too. You can paste the path into the folder box.

    extension

Done: the UDM button is in the toolbar's puzzle-piece menu. Pin it to keep it in sight. Downloads now go to Downloads\udm.

Keep the extension folder where it is: the browser loads UDM from there. After an update, press the reload arrow on UDM on the extensions page.
